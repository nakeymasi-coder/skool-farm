How this farm works

This dashboard runs your Skool community The Glam Vault (https://www.skool.com/the-glam-vault-9512): planning community posts, building Classroom lessons, designing challenges, keeping members talking and celebrated, planning Work Hours, and keeping reusable resources in one place. After you submit a job, your AI runs it WITH you in chat and brings the finished post, lesson, or package back for your approval.

Plain honesty: this dashboard is your control panel. It does not access Skool, browse, generate, or post by itself - the work happens in chat from the Prepare in Chat prompts. NOTHING is ever posted automatically. Posting to Skool always needs your separate approval.

The 5 specialists, in order

  • 1. Community Content Planner: plans and drafts community posts - prompt posts, announcements, encouragement, celebrations, spotlights - in your voice, each with a title, full body, category pick, and one clear call to action.
  • 2. Work Hours Coordinator: plans your Tuesday and Thursday 6 to 7 PM ET Work Hours sessions - turns a topic and member requests into an ordered build-along agenda with timing and a short prep list.
  • 3. Lesson & Tutorial Builder: builds Classroom lesson packages for Live Resources - lesson title, folder title (dated pattern, 50 characters max), what members learn, steps in order, any resource links, plus the announcement post that points members to it.
  • 4. Creative Challenge Designer: designs member challenges - plain rules, how to enter by posting, daily or step prompts, and how winners get celebrated (only the recognition you actually enter - never invent a prize).
  • 5. Engagement Assistant: drafts welcomes, wins celebrations, encouragement, and conversation starters, and replies in your voice. If a job needs an actual member comment or post and you did not paste it, the job saves as "Needs details" in its prompt - never invent what a member said.

The 7 content types

  • 1. Announcement / New Drop - home category: ANNOUNCEMENTS.
  • 2. Prompt Post - home category: PROMPTS.
  • 3. Freebie / Resource Drop - home category: FREEBIES, or a Live Resources lesson.
  • 4. Lesson / Tutorial - Classroom - Live Resources.
  • 5. Engagement / Conversation Starter - home category: THE HANGOUT.
  • 6. Encouragement / Celebration - home category: Words Of Encouragement or Celebrate Your Accomplishments.

My real Skool categories

Feed categories: THE HANGOUT, PROMPTS, ANNOUNCEMENTS, $2 DOLLAR TUESDAYS, FREEBIES, IMAGES, Words Of Encouragement, Celebrate Your Accomplishments, Ready To Sell Products. Every category choice on every form also offers None.

Classroom note: Live Resources is a Classroom course, not a feed category. Lessons go there, in dated folders named "MM/DD/YY - Topic". Skool folder names cap at 50 characters, so suggested folder titles stay at or under 50 characters.

Inside The Glam Vault, member content is free for members: no buy talk, no prices, no shop links in Vault posts.

The Skool standing order

A Skool post is not done until it has a category, one clear call to action, and - when it points somewhere - a working destination link. Inside The Glam Vault there is no buy talk.

Your Skool connection

How Prepare in Chat works

Every Queue card gets a Prepare in Chat button. Pressing it sets that job to "working" and opens a card showing every field of that job, plus a COPY PROMPT button. The copied prompt tells the AI to do THAT exact job with THOSE exact details, nothing swapped, in your voice, and to bring the finished work back in chat for your approval. When you approve a post or lesson, the prompt also tells the AI to carry it into Skool on the signed-in browser connection and read it back to prove it landed. There is no scheduled pickup.

Queue status buttons only change the label - queued, working, or done. Publication status is separate: Draft, Approved, or Published and verified, and "Published and verified" only saves when a live link and a verification date are both filled in. Changing a status never posts anything.

Character Vault - locked characters

    
One record per locked character. Slot 1 is the headshot, one photo, 9:16. Slot 2 is the body reference sheet: ONE single 9:16 photo - not separate photos - with front, three-quarter, left profile, and right side views of the same person, same outfit, same background, all on that one photo. A character is NOT locked until BOTH slots are filled. Uploads: any image your browser can open. The farm reads the file directly with FileReader, shrinks a copy in the browser to a storage-sized JPG (max 1080px) when that works, and if the shrink step fails it keeps the photo exactly as read. A readable photo is never rejected. AI-created images are still PNG only. Characters are ultra realistic, with real skin texture and real hair texture. Every character is African American unless a specific existing character was named. Plus-size stays plus-size, never slimmed down. Within one video, identity, clothing, location, and lighting stay frozen from the reference photos.
