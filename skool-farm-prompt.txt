SKOOL FARM v2 - THE SKOOL-FIRST BUILD PROMPT (Glam's Skool edition, rebuilt)
Version 2, at Glam's direction on 2026-10-31: "instead of it being UGC
focused I need it to be more Skool focused." This version supersedes the
v1 UGC-first prompt (kept as skool-farm-prompt-v1-ugc-backup.txt). The
Skool-first pattern is Glam's own Glam Vault Community Command Center
(built 2026-10-25): community work at the center, specialists doing the
work, video demoted to a small side tool for lesson videos only.
================================================================================
Use this prompt to build / rebuild the dashboard. Where it conflicts with
any default - including the old farm master prompt - THIS prompt wins.

THE DASHBOARD
Build a working SKOOL FARM dashboard - a real tool I can use, not a
document, not a lesson, not an explanation of one. ONE self-contained
interactive page (HTML) that runs when opened. It keeps its own name,
icon set, and install manifest: name "Skool Farm - Glam Hustle Hub",
short name "Skool Farm", manifest.webmanifest, icon-512.png,
icon-192.png, apple-touch-icon.png, favicon.png (unchanged from v1).

WHAT THIS FARM IS
One dashboard runs my Skool community, The Glam Vault
(https://www.skool.com/the-glam-vault-9512): planning community posts,
building Classroom lessons, designing challenges, keeping members talking
and celebrated, planning Work Hours, and keeping reusable resources in
one place. Five specialists do the work (below). After I submit a job, my
AI runs it WITH me in chat and brings the finished post, lesson, or
package back for my approval. NOTHING is ever posted automatically. Say
that plainly inside the dashboard: the dashboard is my control panel; it
does not access Skool, browse, generate, or post by itself - the work
happens in chat from the Prepare in Chat prompts.
SKOOL CONNECTION (write this on Home, plainly): "Your Skool is connected
through the signed-in browser. Connection checked Oct 31, 2026: signed in,
The Glam Vault opens in admin view. When a post or lesson is ready and
you approve it, your AI can carry it into Skool on that connection and
read it back to prove it landed. Nothing is posted without your OK."

DESIGN
Clean, pretty, phone-first. Light sapphire blue (#7fb2e8 family), chrome
silver, and pearl white, soft ombre gradients, small butterfly touches.
Big readable text, big tap-friendly buttons. START EMPTY for a new user:
no test members, posts, lessons, resources, or sample rows anywhere.
The dashboard saves in the browser with localStorage key skoolFarmV1 -
THE SAME KEY AS V1, and the store shape grows, never resets:
{characters: [], queue: [], resources: []}. A v1 save ({characters,
queue} with no resources) MUST load with every character and queue item
preserved; v1 queue cards render with whatever fields they have.

PAGES (tabs, in this order)
HOME: explains the farm, the 5 specialists, the 7 content types, my real
categories, Work Hours, the standing order, the Skool connection note,
and how Prepare in Chat works.
COMMUNITY INTAKE: the full community job form (fields below).
SPECIALISTS: one solo form per specialist (5 forms).
RESOURCES: my reusable resources vault (add / list / copy link).
VIDEO TOOL: the small side tool - Character Vault plus a lesson-video
run form (the old UGC machinery, shrunk to one tab).
QUEUE: every intake job, specialist job, and video run in one list.

SKOOL FACTS TO BAKE IN (real, do not invent others)
- Community: The Glam Vault, https://www.skool.com/the-glam-vault-9512
  (39 members at last check, Oct 31, 2026 - do not hard-code member
  counts anywhere in copy the farm produces).
- Feed categories: THE HANGOUT, PROMPTS, ANNOUNCEMENTS, $2 DOLLAR
  TUESDAYS, FREEBIES, IMAGES, Words Of Encouragement, Celebrate Your
  Accomplishments, Ready To Sell Products.
- Classroom: Live Resources is a Classroom course, not a feed category.
  Lessons go there, in dated folders named "MM/DD/YY - Topic" (Skool
  folder names cap at 50 characters - keep suggested folder titles at or
  under 50 characters and say so in the lesson prompt).
- Work Hours: Tuesday and Thursday, 6 to 7 PM ET, build-along sessions.
- Inside The Glam Vault, member content is free for members: no buy talk,
  no prices, no shop links in Vault posts.

THE 5 SPECIALISTS - in this order:
1. COMMUNITY CONTENT PLANNER: plans and drafts community posts - prompt
   posts, announcements, encouragement, celebrations, spotlights - in my
   voice, each with a title, full body, category pick, and one clear call
   to action.
2. WORK HOURS COORDINATOR: plans Tue/Thu 6-7 PM ET Work Hours sessions -
   turns a topic and member requests into an ordered build-along agenda
   with timing and a short prep list.
3. LESSON & TUTORIAL BUILDER: builds Classroom lesson packages for Live
   Resources - lesson title, folder title (dated pattern, 50 characters
   max), what members learn, steps in order, any resource links, plus the
   announcement post that points members to it.
4. CREATIVE CHALLENGE DESIGNER: designs member challenges - plain rules,
   how to enter by posting, daily or step prompts, and how winners get
   celebrated (only the recognition I actually enter - never invent a
   prize).
5. ENGAGEMENT ASSISTANT: drafts welcomes, wins celebrations,
   encouragement, and conversation starters, and replies in my voice. If
   a job needs an actual member comment or post and I did not paste it,
   the job saves as "Needs details" in its prompt - never invent what a
   member said.

THE 7 CONTENT TYPES (the intake's job types; each maps to a home
category, and every form category offers a None option):
1. Announcement / New Drop - ANNOUNCEMENTS
2. Prompt Post - PROMPTS
3. Freebie / Resource Drop - FREEBIES or a Live Resources lesson
4. Lesson / Tutorial - Classroom - Live Resources
5. Engagement / Conversation Starter - THE HANGOUT
6. Encouragement / Celebration - Words Of Encouragement or Celebrate
   Your Accomplishments
7. Product Spotlight - Ready To Sell Products (inside the Vault: shown
   and celebrated, no buy talk)

COMMUNITY INTAKE FORM - fields in this exact order:
1. Content Type (the 7 above)
2. Specialist to assign (None, or one of the 5 specialists)
3. Skool Category (None, Classroom - Live Resources, or one of my 9 real
   categories)
4. Topic
5. What members get (the post, lesson, or resource, in plain words)
6. Destination link, when there is one
7. Reference link, when there is one
8. Notes

SOLO SPECIALIST FORMS - one per specialist, each producing a clean
ready-to-copy prompt prefilled from what I entered, and each saving its
job to the Queue:
1. Content Planner: topic; Skool category (full list + None); post goal;
   notes.
2. Work Hours Coordinator: session day (Tuesday, Thursday, or None);
   session topic; member requests (pasted); notes.
3. Lesson Builder: lesson topic; what members learn; destination or
   resource link; notes.
4. Challenge Designer: challenge theme; how many days or steps; how
   members enter; recognition (exactly what I type, may be blank);
   Skool category (full list + None); notes.
5. Engagement Assistant: thread type (Welcome, Wins celebration,
   Encouragement, Conversation starter, Reply to a member, or None);
   the member's post or comment pasted (may be blank - then the prompt
   says Needs details); Skool category (full list + None); notes.

RESOURCES (Resource Vault): a simple add form - title; type (Prompt,
Guide or Code, App Link, Replay, Freebie, Lesson, Other, or None); link;
notes. Resources list newest first with a copy-link button. Resources
are stored under "resources" in the same store. They are not Queue jobs.

VIDEO TOOL (one tab, small on purpose): this is the only place the old
UGC machinery lives, for lesson videos and video posts.
- CHARACTER VAULT: one record per character - letter and name, voice
  description; SLOT 1 HEADSHOT (one photo, 9:16); SLOT 2 BODY REFERENCE
  SHEET (ONE single 9:16 photo, front + three-quarter + left profile +
  right side, same outfit, same background). NOT LOCKED until both slots
  are filled - say so on the card. Uploads: read the file directly with
  FileReader, shrink a copy in-browser to a storage-sized JPG (max
  1080px) when that works, keep the photo exactly as read when the shrink
  fails - a readable photo is never rejected, accept="image/*".
  Characters are ultra realistic, real skin and hair texture, African
  American unless I named a specific existing character, plus-size stays
  plus-size.
- VIDEO RUN FORM: locked character picker (or create new); Product
  Presentation right after the character pick (None, Wearing, Holding,
  Using, Showing close to camera, Sitting beside her, Unboxing, Holding
  up by her face, Holding in some scenes + wearing in others - the full 9,
  None first, each with its exact scene sentence); topic; length;
  product name and link; notes. Video runs save to the same Queue.
- THE SCENE PROMPT SHAPE and workflow live in the video run's copied
  prompt only: "Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly
  consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.]" plus the one
  presentation sentence for the choice (None adds none). Never describe
  the character in a scene prompt. Workflow: outfit picked on Pinterest
  in guest view, locked in chat on the character, outfit sheet made in
  chat as one PNG, video built from Reference One (headshot) + Reference
  Two (outfit sheet, never empty), returned in chat for approval, never
  posted.

QUEUE: all jobs together, newest first. Each card shows: job kind
(Intake / specialist name / Video), content type when there is one,
Skool category, topic, destination link, and TWO separate manual
statuses: run status (queued / working / done - buttons change the label
only) and publication status (Draft / Approved / Published and verified -
a manual select; choosing "Published and verified" reveals live link and
verification date fields, and it saves as published only when both are
filled). V1 queue items (which have mode / presentation fields instead)
render on the same card style with the fields they have.

PREPARE IN CHAT: every Queue card gets a Prepare in Chat button. It sets
the run status to "working" and opens a card showing every field of that
job, plus a COPY PROMPT button. The copied prompt tells the AI to do THAT
exact job with THOSE exact details, nothing swapped, in my voice, ending
by bringing the finished work back in chat for my approval - and, when I
approve a post or lesson, carrying it into Skool on the signed-in browser
connection and reading it back to prove it landed. Video prompts carry
the video workflow and scene shape from the Video Tool section. There is
no scheduled pickup. Do not build one.

STANDING ORDER, baked into Home and every copied prompt:
"A Skool post is not done until it has a category, one clear call to
action, and - when it points somewhere - a working destination link.
Inside The Glam Vault there is no buy talk."

STANDING RULES:
- Nothing is posted automatically, ever. Posting to Skool always needs my
  separate approval, and a published item is only marked Published and
  verified after its live link is read back.
- No price, discount, or sale claim unless a live price was actually
  verified. No buy talk inside The Glam Vault.
- No Payhip anywhere. Links point to glamhustlehub.com or to Skool.
- Every image my AI creates is PNG. Uploaded Vault photos are read as I
  saved them and never rejected when readable.
- Never invent a member, a member's words, a prize, or a metric.
- Voice and copy everywhere: personable, human, plain words. No em
  dashes - use hyphens instead.
